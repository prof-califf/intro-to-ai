Intro to AI in Business

Eleven weeks, eleven real systems, one future — a drug-hunter, a robotaxi, a résumé screen, a feed that wants you back — and one question underneath all of them: can we build a good, beneficial hybrid society? This book teaches a way of looking, not a side to take. See it. Question it. Decide.

A free, critical field guide to AI — for the people who'll live with it, not just build it.

The five questions we ask every week

1What is it, and what does it do?
2Why does it exist, and who benefits?
3What happens when it fails, and who finds out?
4Who's saying that, and how would we know?
5Keep it, fix it, or drop it, and what does that cost?

The only thing this course asks you to memorize. Three to see the system, one to test any claim about it, one to decide.

How this book works

The weekly rhythm

Before Monday, read the chapter. That's the whole ask. Come with one thing you didn't buy.

Monday opens with a five-minute reading quiz, then we talk it through: the case, the five questions, where you landed.

Wednesday is the lab: a short recap, then you work, in pairs, laptop open, then we talk about what you found. Every lab has you doing something real, often with a live AI on the other side.

Friday night the lab writeup is due. Its last line is your commitment card, one sentence you actually keep.
