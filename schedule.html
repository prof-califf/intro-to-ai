The Schedule

One chapter a week, one rhythm all quarter. Read the chapter over the weekend. Discussion Monday. Lab Wednesday. Writeup and quiz Friday night. Eleven weeks, eleven systems, one question the whole way through.

First class Wed Sept 23 · midterm Wed Oct 21 · Prototype project due Fri Dec 4 · final exam Thu Dec 10

The weekly rhythm · every week unless marked

Weekend: read the chapter. Nothing is due.

Monday: the chapter's case, how it works, and the five questions, then discussion.

Wednesday: a short recap of Monday, then the lab, in class, in pairs, then a debate on the question the lab raises. The lab ends with a commitment card.

Friday, 11:59 p.m.: the five-item lab writeup and the five-question chapter quiz, both on Canvas.

Things worth knowing

  • Read the chapter over the weekend. Monday is the case and the discussion, and it assumes you have read it. Nothing is ever due on a weekend.
  • Labs are done in pairs, writeups are your own. Same work, your own words, five items, due Friday at 11:59 p.m. on Canvas.
  • You are never required to use an AI tool to complete a lab, a chapter, or the Prototype project.
  • Lowest lab writeup and lowest quiz are dropped. One bad week costs nothing and needs no explanation.
  • Chapter 8, Ethics and War, sits in Veterans Day week on purpose. It is a Monday-only week, with the lab started in class that day and the holiday held with the respect it deserves.
  • The book will change during the quarter. AI moves fast enough that some of it will be out of date by December. When that happens I update the chapter and say what changed.
  • Dates can shift. Canvas is the authority if anything here disagrees with it.
